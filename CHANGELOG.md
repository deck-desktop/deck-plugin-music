# Changelog

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-19)

- The player can sit in Deck's footer (Settings > Plugins > Music > Footer): play/pause and the track's
  title, which goes round in a fixed width while it plays, and a click opens the player as the
  footer's menu, with the cover, seek bar, controls, volume and Listen together. In a narrow window
  the title gives way first and play/pause stays. Needs Deck 1.18.7.
- The Music tab fills its pane, without the margin around it.
- Switching from the floating player to the bar no longer leaves the bar where the card had been
  dragged.

## 1.4.1 (2026-11-12)

- Listen together asks again for a track that stalled, instead of staying silent.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-12)

- Listen together: invite a Deck team, or one person in it, to hear what you play as you play it.
  Whoever invited controls playback; each listener has their own volume. Asks for the network
  permission.
- Play on the track already loaded starts it again.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-01)

- Runs on Linux: downloads use python3 and the folder's own separator. Needs Deck 1.15.0.

## 1.2.1 (2026-10-30)

- A licence, this changelog, a README rewritten with a screenshot, and the permissions it uses
  declared. Built from its own lockfile.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-18)

- The first version in Deck's plugin registry.
